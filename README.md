# 📊 Madhav E-Commerce Sales Dashboard | Power BI

An interactive **Power BI E-Commerce Sales Dashboard** developed to analyze sales performance, profitability, customer behavior, product performance, regional sales, and payment methods.

## 🎯 Project Objective

The objective of this project is to provide an executive-level view of Madhav Store's performance and identify key revenue drivers, profitable product segments, regional sales patterns, and loss-making areas.

## 📌 Dashboard Overview

The dashboard contains two main pages:

### 1. Executive Sales Dashboard

Provides an interactive view of:

* Total Revenue
* Net Profit
* Profit Margin
* Units Sold
* Average Order Value
* Monthly Profit Trends
* Category & Sub-Category Performance
* Payment Mode Analysis
* State-wise Sales
* Top Customers

### 2. Overall View & Insights

The executive insights page highlights important business findings from the analysis.

## 🔎 Key Business Insights

* Maharashtra and Madhya Pradesh together contribute more than **43% of total revenue**.
* Clothing is the largest volume category, contributing **62.6% of total unit sales**.
* Printers and Bookcases are among the strongest highlighted profit-generating sub-categories.
* Furnishings and Electronic Games show negative profit in the analyzed segments.
* COD and UPI together represent more than **64% of customer checkouts**.
* May records a highlighted monthly net loss of **₹3,730**.
